FAQ

Questions, answered

Does OffRoad really work without internet?

Yes. You need a connection once, to download a model from Hugging Face. After that, chatting, image questions and voice all run on your phone with no network at all, including in airplane mode.

Is anything I type sent to a server?

No. The model runs on your device and conversations are saved in a database inside the app. There are no accounts, analytics or advertising SDKs. The only network traffic is the model downloads you start.

Which phones are supported?

iPhones on iOS 16 or later and Android phones on Android 11 or later (64-bit). Small models like Qwen2.5 0.5B run on almost anything; larger ones such as Gemma 4 E4B want a recent flagship with 6 GB+ RAM. Each model lists its requirements in the app.

How much storage do models take?

From about 140 MB (SmolLM 135M) to 6.3 GB (Gemma 3n E4B). The Storage tab shows exactly what each model uses, and you can delete one at any time; your conversations are kept.

Why do some models need a Hugging Face token?

A few publishers, including Google for some Gemma models, require you to accept their licence first. Accept it on huggingface.co, create a free read token, and paste it in Settings. It's stored in your phone's secure keychain and only ever sent to Hugging Face.

Is OffRoad free?

Yes, OffRoad is free to download and use. The models are open-weight releases from their publishers.
